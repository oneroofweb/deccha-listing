About This Business

Greenfields Gardens is a premier wedding venue and event management company located in the heart of Rajaji Puram, Lucknow. Established in 2015, we have hosted over 500 successful weddings and corporate events.

Our lush green lawns and air-conditioned banquet halls provide the perfect setting for your special day. We offer end-to-end wedding planning services including catering, decoration, photography, and artist management.
